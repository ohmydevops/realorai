Create a professional AI portrait of yourself

Turn a clear photo of yourself into a natural professional headshot for your CV, work profile or personal website—with a prompt that protects your likeness.

Start with a good photograph

Use a recent, sharp head-and-shoulders photo with your whole face visible. Face a window for soft daylight, keep the camera at eye level and avoid beauty filters, wide-angle close-ups and harsh shadows. Upload only your own photo or one you have permission to use; check the service’s photo storage and deletion settings.

Write a specific prompt

Upload your photograph to an image editor that accepts reference images. Describe the purpose, framing, clothing, background and lighting. Ask it to preserve your identity; a prompt cannot guarantee an exact likeness.

Prompt to copy

Edit the attached photo into a realistic professional head-and-shoulders portrait for my work profile. Preserve my identity, facial proportions, age, skin tone, hairline and distinctive features. Keep my natural skin texture and expression. Use soft window light, a simple neutral background and a plain dark jacket over a solid-color shirt. Keep the camera at eye level and the pose relaxed. Do not reshape my face, beautify my features, smooth my skin excessively or add glossy eyes. Keep lighting and shadows physically consistent. No text or logos.

Refine and check the result

Compare the output with the original at phone size and close up. Check eyes, teeth, ears, hair, glasses and clothing edges. Request one change at a time, such as a quieter background. Reject results that change your face. Keep the original, export for the intended platform and describe the image as AI-edited where disclosure is required. This portrait is for profiles, not proof of identity.
