Turn a simple selfie into a formal photo

Use a clear selfie and a good English prompt to create a neat, natural photo for a profile, CV or internal badge.

Where could you use it?

A simple portrait with a clean background can work for a professional profile, CV, personal website or some internal badges. If you need it for a particular document or organization, read its photo requirements once before using it.

Take a suitable selfie

Face a window and hold a clean camera lens at eye level, a little away from your face. Turn off beauty filters and portrait blur. Keep your head straight and your expression natural. Leave room around your head and shoulders and save the original.

Upload your photo and use this prompt

Edit the attached selfie into a natural, formal head-and-shoulders photo. Preserve my identity, facial geometry, age, skin tone, hairline and distinctive features. Keep my face recognizable and my skin texture natural. Use an eye-level composition, a plain light background and soft, even lighting with realistic shadows. Keep my head straight and my expression neutral. Use a simple, solid-color shirt. Do not reshape my face, add makeup, exaggerate symmetry or invent hidden facial details. Leave space around my head and shoulders for cropping. No text, stamps, logos or document graphics.

Refine the result

Use an editor that accepts a reference photo. After the prompt, request one change at a time, such as a whiter background or a plain navy shirt. If the angle or face looks wrong, provide a better selfie.

Check before saving

Compare with the original: eyes, hair, ears and face shape should still look like you. Check hair and clothing edges and lighting. Crop for the intended use, export at suitable resolution and keep the original.
