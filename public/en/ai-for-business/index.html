AI images for your business: a quick guide

Use AI image generation to create Instagram posts, covers, posters, and promotional images for your business. Learn how to choose tools, write better prompts, and keep your brand style consistent.

What can you make?

Post backgrounds, Stories, Reel covers, website banners, posters, flyers, and thank-you cards. For product promotions, start with a real product photo and change the background.

Which tools can you use?

  • ChatGPT Images
  • Google Gemini
  • Midjourney
  • Adobe Firefly
  • Ideogram

How do you write a better prompt?

A prompt is the description you give the tool. Specify the purpose, subject and setting, lighting and camera angle, colors and style, and space for text.

For example: “Create a natural-looking photo for a café post: a coffee cup on a wooden table, soft light from the left, a table-level camera angle, and cream and green colors. Leave the top clear for a headline. No text or logos.”

After reviewing the result, request one specific change at a time, such as “Simplify the background.” Add your logo, prices, and final wording in a design editor.

Choose the right dimensions

Posts, Stories, and covers use different dimensions. Choose the size and aspect ratio for the placement, and specify square, portrait, or landscape in your prompt. Keep important details away from the edges and preview the crop. For printing, ask your printer for the required size and resolution, and check a sample first.

Keep a consistent palette and style

Save a palette with color codes: your main brand color, one or two supporting colors, and a neutral. For example, repeat green in borders or signature details and use cream for backgrounds. Keep lighting, camera angles, and headline and logo placement consistent too.

Keep the palette and a few approved designs in one folder. In chats outside a shared project, upload the palette and a previous example, explaining what should stay consistent; do not assume the tool can access earlier files. If exact colors matter, correct them in a design editor.

Save shared context in a ChatGPT project

Create a separate project for each brand or ongoing job. Add your palette, logo, and previous examples to its files or sources. In project instructions, describe your audience, colors, and style: “Use the attached palette, natural lighting, simple backgrounds, and space for headlines.” Start new chats inside that project to use its shared files and instructions without repeating the same brief.

Check before publishing

Inspect the image close up and at phone size. Is the text correct and readable? Do lighting, shadows, textures, and proportions look natural? Are perspective, hands, reflections, and connections between objects believable? Fix or regenerate mistakes.

Compare the product with your original photograph, and do not present generated images as real photographs of your products, premises, or service results. Check the tool’s commercial-use terms too.
